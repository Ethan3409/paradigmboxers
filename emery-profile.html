Health & Testing

Comprehensive health screening exceeding standard protocols. Emery is the example of healthy, inside and out!

1. Echocardiogram

Clear. Results: Aortic Vmax 1.63 m/s, Pulmonary Vmax 1.23 m/s. Heart health is paramount.

2. 24-Hour Holter

Score of 1, 0, 0. A fantastic 24-hour heart monitoring result ensuring no arrhythmias.

3. DNA Testing (ARVC & DM)

N/N by parentage (both mom and dad are N/N- so she can only be N/N) for ARVC1, ARVC2, and Degenerative Myelopathy. Genetic soundness guaranteed.

4. OFA Hips & Elbows

Hips rated Good, Elbows rated Normal. Excellent structural health and joint consistency.

5. SDMA Lab Testing

11 Normal SDMA. Advanced screening for early detection of kidney function health.

6. Thyroid Panel

Complete metabolic and endocrine soundness verified through professional panel testing- NORMAL
