Introducing

Mickey

Mickey embodies a classic, expressive Boxer temperament—lively, confident, and deeply connected to the people around him. He carries a bright, engaging nature that stands out without tipping into excess. His energy is present, but it’s moderate and purposeful, giving him a spirited character that remains easy to live with when guided well.

He responds strongly to routine and clear expectations. With consistency, Mickey channels his enthusiasm into focus, cooperation, and a growing steadiness. As he matures, he continues to show more composure and reliability while keeping the spark that makes him unmistakably Boxer. This balance of spirit and stability is increasingly uncommon in the breed and is part of what makes him such a valuable addition to the Paradigm program.

Mickey’s temperament reflects the qualities we aim to preserve: a dog who is expressive, willing, and engaged, yet grounded enough to be a pleasure to work with. His developing maturity and well‑rounded character position him to produce puppies with authentic, balanced Boxer temperament.
