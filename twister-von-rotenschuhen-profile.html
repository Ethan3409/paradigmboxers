Twister Von Rotenschuhen

Eko Del Colle Dell'Infinito x Wannabe Yvonne Del Coure Grande

Twister is a powerful, athletic male with excellent balance, a clear and social temperament, and a strong work ethic. Imported from Europe, he combines proven structure and health with a stable, biddable character, making him a valuable addition to the Paradigm breeding program.

Twister is sired by Eko Del Colle Dell’Infinito, a World Champion and a Vetran World Champion; the producer of several Champions. Beyond his exceptional accomplishments in the show ring and as a sire, Eko also demonstrates the longevity we value within our pedigrees. He remains in Europe and, at 11 years of age, continues to represent the strength, soundness, and longevity behind Twister’s bloodline.
